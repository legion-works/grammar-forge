GRAMMARFORGE · IDENTITY

A mark of its own

GrammarForge is a product built with Legion Works — so its mark is a sibling to the Legion aperture, not a copy of it. Same molten cyan core, same glow, same restraint. Its own symbol: the proofreader's caret, its point forged white-hot, resting on a line of text marked up in the four issue-category colors. Grammar and Forge in one glyph.

Self-hosted writing assistant. Spelling · grammar · punctuation · style — checked locally, in your editor, your terminal, your chat.

▸ THREE DIRECTIONS

My pick is 1a — it carries the most meaning at the smallest size and reads instantly as "editing." Say the word and I'll lock in 1b or 1c everywhere instead.

1a Recommended
The forge caret

The proofreader's insertion caret — also the terminal ^ — its point forged to a molten core, over the four category underline ticks. Symbol + product signature in one.

1b
The forged G

A lettermark: a geometric G, its inner bar ending in the molten core. Ownable and friendly, but a letter, not a concept — it says less about what the product does.

1c
The underline

Literally the product: three lines of text, each word underlined in its category color, a live cursor blinking. Truest to the UI — but busiest, and softest at favicon size.
